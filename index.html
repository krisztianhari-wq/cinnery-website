About us

Two best friends, one oven

Welcome to Cinnery! We are two childhood best friends who share a lifelong passion for baking, cooking and, above all, creating moments of pure joy through food.

Driven by our love for sweet treats, we founded Cinnery with a simple mission: to bring the warmth, comfort and undeniable magic of authentic, homemade baking to everyone.

Every single roll and cookie we make is crafted with love, high-quality ingredients and that cosy, home-cooked feeling. We can't wait to share our passion with you, one sweet bite at a time!

Yes! Order and pay online through our pickup ordering page and collect your rolls at Zwanestraat 29 at the time you choose. For catering, large orders or B2B requests, please email us at cinnery.nl@gmail.com.
How do I store and reheat my cinnamon rolls?

Best enjoyed fresh, but if you want to save some joy for later:

  • Storage: airtight container, up to 2 days at room temperature or up to 4 days in the fridge.
  • Freezing: wrap tightly and freeze for up to 1 month. Thaw at room temperature for 20–30 minutes before reheating.

Reheat for that perfect gooey texture:

  • Microwave (quickest): 800 W for about 30 seconds.
  • Oven (oven-fresh taste): 160 °C, wrap loosely in foil, 5–7 minutes.
  • Air fryer (crispy edge): 150 °C for 2–3 minutes.
