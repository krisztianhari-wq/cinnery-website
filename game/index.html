Help Us – Roll Run

Help our baker collect everything a cinnamon roll needs – from Amsterdam’s canals via The Hague to our bakery in Groningen.

Tap, press Space or ↑ to jump. Hold for a higher jump.

Taste it for real

How to play

The baker runs by herself – you only jump. Tap the game, press Space or the up arrow; hold it down to jump higher. Bump into something and a few ingredients fall out of her bag, but the run always goes on.

Look out for parked bikes, pigeons, seagulls, puddles and canals, cobblestones and trams. Tulip boxes and trams are safe to land on.

The ingredients

Every roll needs one of each: flour, sugar, butter, egg, yeast and cinnamon. Special ingredients decide the flavour that comes out of the machine:

  • cream cheese – Classic Cream Cheese
  • cocoa – Red Velvet
  • Biscoff biscuits – Biscoff
  • pistachios – Pistachio
  • caramel and pecans – Salted Caramel Pecan
  • blueberries – Blueberry Crumble

No special ingredient? Then it’s a Classic Naked. The real ones are baked fresh every day at Zwanestraat 29, Groningen – see the menu.
